About Us

Get To Know Expressive Model Making Co.

A professional architecture model making career isn't an easy one. It's a full-time commitment requiring personal sacrifice and a dedication to preparation. Expressive Model Making Co. began to form their incredible work ethic at a young age, spending time outside school and on the weekends practising in pursuit of the ultimate goal: to be counted amongst the top models.

A Model's Tale — Bio

Achieving success as a well-renowned Architecture Scale Model Making was no walk in the park for Expressive Model Making Co.. They worked incredibly hard to perfect their modelling style, developing a genuine and unique persona both in front of and behind the camera. Always eager for new opportunities to diversify an already impressive portfolio, Expressive Model Making Co. is constantly seeking to work with new clients. If you're interested in learning more about Expressive Model Making Co., please don't hesitate to get in touch at expressivemodel@gmail.com.

Headquartered in Byculla (E), Mumbai, near Rani Baug, our studio merges traditional artisan handcraft with high-power laser acrylic cutting machines and advanced industrial 3D printing equipment.

Mumbai Workshop Heritage

Artisan Mastery Meets Digital Fabrication

Every project at Expressive Model Making Co. begins with rigorous CAD and blueprint checking. Our skilled craftsmen carefully scale every beam, mullion, staircase, and balustrade before feeding geometries into high-precision laser cutters and resin 3D printers.

The pieces are then hand-assembled, detailed, weathered, and illuminated by our master model makers in Byculla, Mumbai. All models are delivered with museum-grade acrylic display hoods and protective crates.
